4. Why the loop eventually kills it

The circle is perfectly stable — until the local picture changes. Two things break it:

a) It eats. The body grows one segment, the tail stops vacating, and a cell the loop needs is suddenly BLOCKED: your own body. The loop has a hole in it and the goldfish has no backup plan.

b) Greed. Food spawns somewhere tempting, one "moves toward food" step pulls it off the loop — into a corner or its own body — and it can't find its way back.

That's the shape of every v2 death: long, boring, competent circling … then one changed snapshot and it's over.

5. The fix: give the goldfish a memory

Last-N moves in the prompt. Append "your last 8 moves: up, right, right, down…" Cheap, and the model can literally see itself repeating.
Visited-cell warning. Track visit counts in the runner; when a cell is hit 4+ times in 40 moves, add "you've been circling — break the pattern."
Runner-side loop breaker. Detect the cycle deterministically (like the analysis above did) and force an exploratory move off the loop.
